On Vijay · ADMK · DMK · BJP · Corruption
And what the hell comes next

01 — The new entrant

Vijay & TVK

I empathise with all the voters who voted for Vijay. But one question lingers: after the catastrophe in Karur (I don't blame him), Vijay stormed out and ran from the media. He took a solid week to speak on the issue, yet it was very poorly articulated.

"nan veetla illana office la iruppen en Mela kai vainga."

Now, with the Jananayagan Leak, there is nothing at all. Every time he has been given a chance to show his grit as a leader, he has come shy of courage and leadership itself. He cannot hide behind his cinema fame and his fans everywhere. All the TN party policies are "freebies maxed," and TVK is no stranger.

When he claims he is the change that TN wants, I am excited — BUT how is he going to do that? He has given MLA seat tickets to approximately 50 people who just switched parties over his loyal cadres. He did take on the ruling party well, but that alone is not enough. Politics is real; he will face criticism from everyone for everything. It's not his movie premiere. Vijay has a clean slate and valuable precedents of what will work and what won't, so I hope he steps up and doesn't become the next Kamal Haasan.

02 — The silver lining

Overlook

I welcome the entry of Vijay because it has put both these outdated Dravidian parties — sluggish, PR-oriented politics in TN — at risk. From now on, whoever comes to power will have to work better.

03 — The old guard

ADMK vs DMK

Honestly, I am not a big fan of both; they lost their identity and dignity over and over again. ADMK still has a democratic say in who is in charge of what, whereas DMK belongs to the single Gopalapuram Family. DMK ridicules civil structures and makes them spineless.

The biggest joke is their pledge for Social Justice. Both (three now) play caste politics without any guilt. ADMK hatred for its alliance with BJP — but the moment you ask why, many struggle to reason it. They would say Hindi imposition, but it was Congress that systematically broke all the languages in the North and made the South believe only Hindi exists there. That reframing deserves more attention — the North is home to Punjabi, Marathi, Bengali, Odia, and dozens of other languages that Congress-era centralisation quietly suffocated. The South was handed a simplified enemy.

Also, calling Hindi an alien language — if we take that route, the DMK family traces back to Telugu origins and MGR and Jayalalithaa are also not Tamil.

04 — The national player

BJP & Opportunism

ALL parties are opportunistic. But BJP has been unapologetic in their schemes and way of politics. They don't follow a nepotistic hierarchy unlike their opposition. What they did very well is polarise the majority that was scattered, and it worked. Now — I want to be honest here — polarising the majority is still identity politics. The difference is that BJP owns it openly while the Dravidian parties dress theirs up as social justice. Both are playing the same game; BJP is just less hypocritical about it.

I would have happily supported TN BJP if it had not formed an alliance with ADMK again because Annamalai deviated from the norm and tried to bring actual change. When the party decided to act against my leader from my state who knows the ground reality, the opportunism is evident. This also validates the general concern of Tamil Nadu losing its autonomy like it did when it gave away Katchatheevu.

Religions were and always will be a major factor in elections. DMK channels minorities whilst BJP unapologetically follows Hindutva principles. This heavily polarised the voting fractions in TN and India itself.

05 — The uncomfortable truth

Corruption & Social Concerns

NONE OF THESE PARTIES ARE NEW TO CORRUPTION. But a few are way bigger than the others and only a few directly affect citizens.

Senthil Balaji
The "10 rupa minister" — cash-for-jobs scandal, arrested 2023.
Raja & Kanimozhi
2G stars — acquittal doesn't erase the scale of what was alleged and the years it took to get there.
Sekar Babu
Extortion allegations that made headlines during the current DMK tenure.

Jayalalithaa was convicted by a Karnataka court in 2014 in a disproportionate assets case worth approximately ₹66 crore — later acquitted on appeal, but the trial ran eighteen years. Karunanidhi faced charges across multiple tenures, most notably the Punjab wheat scam, where allegations of large-scale procurement fraud dogged his administration. Both were great leaders who ruled with great grit; the corruption didn't erase the governance, but it shouldn't be erased from the record either.

Another grey area where DMK thrives is its Gunda culture. Their power is backed by mafias across all domains and it hasn't changed. The drug abuse, illicit liquor deaths, and the spike in POCSO cases were never seen before in TN. The number of bar licenses/TASMAKs opened by this government is 100 times the number of schools and colleges built in its last term. Zero liability to its people, and failed to deliver on anything it promised.

Another thing I hate about DMK is minority appeasement by hurting the majority — this keeps fractions polarised, which they exploit again and again. Now that Vijay came into the picture, this tactic has backfired; minorities see him as their messiah.

06 — The verdict

The Final Ranking

ADMK at least has internal accountability, however imperfect, and a governance track record that EPS has worked to rehabilitate. TVK is an unknown quantity with a leader still proving himself. DMK is a known quantity — and that's the problem.

07 — Who stands out

Figures Worth Watching

S

Seeman — NTK

True to his principles and ideologies even though I am not a fan of his separatist politics. Never once partnered with anyone; always stands alone. Now taking a nationalist approach towards elections — we have to wait.

E

EPS — ADMK

Also an opportunist when he came to power — became CM, sidelined Sasikala, kicked out destabilising elements. His farmers' policies were welcomed. His MoUs with various manufacturing companies (which Stalin sir took credit for), and his work as the "Membalam Minister" — bridge after bridge. His last year was buried under Covid.

A

Annamalai — TN BJP

A man with grit, consciousness, and a clean image stepped in as TN BJP's president. God, he made both the Dravidian parties and BJP itself fear his growth. He knew what would work in TN and how to destabilise them, but it required patience which BJP Center didn't have.

These Dravidians have lived on their ancestors' success of social justice for long enough — it's time they change or leave. We are not here to compare our growth to states behind us; we want to be the state that was an example to countries.

Tamil Nadu is for Tamil and its culture.
